## Detailed Style Profiles

### 1. Modern Dark (`modern-dark`)
- **Philosophy:** Restrained, linear-grade developer software aesthetics.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#050506`, Surface `#090A0F`, Primary `#5E6AD2`, Accent `#8B5CF6`, Border `#1E2230`.
- **Typography:** Geist Sans / Inter display, JetBrains Mono telemetry.
- **Geometry:** 6px radius, 1px thin hairline borders, diffused dark ambient drop shadows.
- **Interaction:** Micro-glow hover states, 150ms cubic-bezier transitions.

### 2. Swiss Minimalist (`swiss-minimalist`)
- **Philosophy:** International Typographic Style (Max Bill, Josef Müller-Brockmann).
- **Palette:** Canvas `#FFFFFF`, Surface `#F7F7F7`, Text `#111111`, Primary `#FF3B00` (International Orange), Border `#E0E0E0`.
- **Typography:** Strict Grotesk sans-serif with rigid baseline grid cadence.
- **Geometry:** 0px sharp right angles, zero decorative shadows, functional structural rule lines.
- **Interaction:** Instant binary color inversions without synthetic ease-in easing.

### 3. Neo-Brutalist Pop (`neo-brutalist`)
- **Philosophy:** Unapologetic physical brutalism contrasting stark black frames with neon energy.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#FFFDF5`, Border `#0A0A0A`, Primary `#FFE600`, Secondary `#00F0FF`, Accent `#FF5E5B`.
- **Typography:** Heavyweight Grotesk display headers with tight tracking.
- **Geometry:** 0px sharp rectangular cards paired with 2.5px heavy black borders and 4px zero-blur hard drop shadows.
- **Interaction:** Tactile button depression: `translate(-2px, -2px)` with shadow expansion.

### 4. Cyberpunk Terminal Deck (`cyber-deck`)
- **Philosophy:** Tactical military cyber-deck HUD and high-frequency telemetry matrices.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#05080F`, Primary Phosphor `#00FF9D`, Laser Coral `#FF0055`, Border `#0E2822`.
- **Typography:** Pure monospaced telemetry headers (Share Tech Mono / JetBrains Mono).
- **Geometry:** 2px micro-chamfered corners with phosphor ambient halo glows.
- **Interaction:** CRT scanline flicker, phosphor blooms, instantaneous command terminal response.

### 5. Spatial Glass Aurora (`glass-aurora`)
- **Philosophy:** Multi-layer translucent spatial computing surfaces floating over cosmic gradients.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#070913`, Primary `#8B5CF6`, Cyan Flare `#06B6D4`, Glass Border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Typography:** Ultra-clean geometric sans with generous optical letter spacing.
- **Geometry:** 14px to 20px generous glass corners, 24px backdrop blur filters, multi-layer specular highlights.
- **Interaction:** Specular highlight shifts following cursor movement, smooth 300ms glass fades.

### 6. Corporate Clean SaaS (`corporate-clean`)
- **Philosophy:** Enterprise-grade reliability, compliance clarity, and frictionless data density.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#F8FAFC`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Primary `#2563EB`, Text `#0F172A`, Border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter engineered for rapid executive reading.
- **Geometry:** Disciplined 6px radius, subtle 1px gray borders, minimal elevation shadows.
- **Interaction:** Subtle background tint hover shifts, high-contrast accessible focus indicators.

### 7. Nordic Frost (`nordic-frost`)
- **Philosophy:** Scandinavian hygge, contemplative open whitespace, and natural organic materials.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#F5F6F8`, Slate `#24272A`, Primary `#24272A`, Accent Terracotta `#C86D51`, Border `#E2E5EA`.
- **Typography:** Balanced neo-grotesk with wide, relaxed paragraph line heights (1.75).
- **Geometry:** 8px soft corners, whisper-soft natural drop shadows (`rgba(36, 39, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Interaction:** Meditative 200ms ease-out transitions evoking natural sunlight and calm focus.

### 8. Midnight Bloom (`midnight-bloom`)
- **Philosophy:** Prestige wealth management, institutional treasury software, and high-trust cryptography.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#030712`, Card `#0B0F19`, Radiant Emerald `#10B981`, Sovereign Gold `#F59E0B`.
- **Typography:** High-legibility sans paired with tabular mono financial numerals.
- **Geometry:** 8px executive radius, 1px slate borders, subtle emerald ambient glow accents.
- **Interaction:** Precision financial value counts, luminescent emerald border highlights.

### 9. Paper & Ink (`paper-ink`)
- **Philosophy:** Fine bookbinding, literary journals, and scholarly thought leadership.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#F7F4EE`, Sumi Ink `#1B1714`, Card `#FAF8F3`, Terracotta Crimson `#A72D23`, Border `#DFD7C7`.
- **Typography:** Classical serif typography hierarchy (Playfair / Georgia / Source Serif).
- **Geometry:** 4px subtle corners, zero heavy drop shadows, delicate hairline deckle borders.
- **Interaction:** Warm sepia underline reveals with contemplative page-turn fade transitions.

### 10. Sunset Synthwave (`sunset-synth`)
- **Philosophy:** 1980s retro-futurist outrun highways, neon supercars, and analog synthesizers.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#0D0221`, Card `#190B38`, Blazing Fuchsia `#FF007F`, Laser Cyan `#00F0FF`, Tangerine `#FF8E3C`.
- **Typography:** Retro-futuristic display fonts (Orbitron / Montserrat) with wide letter tracking.
- **Geometry:** 6px radius with multi-layer colored neon glow shadows.
- **Interaction:** Dual-color neon blooming on hover with high-energy electronic state pulses.

### 11. Claymorphic Soft (`claymorphic-soft`)
- **Philosophy:** Friendly tactile 3D clay volumes, inflated marshmallow surfaces, and comforting physics.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#F8F7F2`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Lavender `#7C3AED`, Coral Peach `#FB7185`, Border `#E8E3DA`.
- **Typography:** Friendly rounded sans-serif (Outfit / Plus Jakarta Sans).
- **Geometry:** 16px to 24px squircle curves, pill action buttons, dual-depth inflated shadows.
- **Interaction:** Bouncy spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`), squishy tap feedback.

### 12. Monochrome Blueprint (`monochrome-wireframe`)
- **Philosophy:** Architectural drafting schematics, structural engineering blueprints, and CAD precision.
- **Palette:** Canvas `#090D16`, Grid Surface `#0E1424`, Blueprint Cyan `#38BDF8`, Construction Amber `#FB923C`.
- **Typography:** Rigid monospaced headers and technical coordinate readouts.
- **Geometry:** 0px sharp rectangular boundaries, 1px technical bounding boxes, zero decorative shadows.
- **Interaction:** Hairline registration crosshairs, instantaneous binary node activation.
